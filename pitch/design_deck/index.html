Slide 01 · Strategic Opportunity

From 9 Fragmented Systems to One Governed Cockpit

Unifying banking data to empower frontline agents, eliminate blind spots, and maximize recovery.
High Impact
[Challenge] The Collections Challenge Today
  • Severe Data Silos: Customer context is fractured across 9 legacy systems (cards, loans, deposits, dialers, CRM, bureau).
  • High Handle Time: Over 3 minutes of a 10-minute call is squandered on manual cross-system record lookup (assumed baseline hypothesis).
  • 40% Broken Promise-to-Pay (PTP): Blanket, impersonal outreach leads to repeat delinquency and friction (assumed baseline).
  • Vulnerability Blind Spots: Hardship disclosures (medical emergencies, job loss) buried in call audio are missed.
[Solution] Our Solution: One Governed Vertical Slice
  • Layer 1 (Data Product Factory): Curates golden Collections 360 (C360) in DuckDB with high-confidence entity matching and data contracts.
  • Layer 2 (Insight & NLP): Collections Ask writes transparent SQL, cites call transcripts via RAG, and refuses out-of-scope queries.
  • Layer 3 (AI Decisioning): Next Best Action (NBA) pairs LightGBM break risk with plain-English SHAP drivers and human override.
  • End-to-End Governance: Zero demographic bias, hardship circuit breakers, and immutable audit logs across all tiers.

Core Innovation: A single, thin, deeply integrated slice from raw data to front-of-house UI. Designed specifically for frontline recovery officers and risk governance executives.

Slide 04 · Governance & Ethics

Responsible AI Built In, Not Bolted On

Scored governance rubric addressed with verifiable technical guarantees and audit trails.
Zero Bias
1. Formal Data Contract

Governed by contracts/data_contract.yaml. Enforces schema constraints, uniqueness, not-null rules, and valid DPD ranges [0, 365]. Emits live pass/fail data quality reports.

2. Zero Demographic Bias

Protected attributes (Age, Gender, Ethnicity, Religion, Marital Status, Postal Code proxies) are mathematically barred from features and models. Verified by automated unit tests.

3. Hardship Circuit Breaker

When vulnerability or hardship is detected from audio transcripts, automated outreach is instantly frozen. Accounts route strictly to human specialists for compassionate review.

4. Read-Only SQL Guardrails
  • Strict SELECT-only AST validation via sqlglot; blocks DDL, DML, and injection.
  • Enforced LIMIT (50 default, 200 max) prevents denial of service.
  • Explicit refusal state detailing why prohibited queries were rejected.
5. Human-in-the-Loop Audit Trail
  • Every recommendation requires explicit human action (Approve or Override).
  • Overrides require mandatory categorization and specialist notes.
  • Tamper-evident audit log records actor, timestamp, inputs, model version, and outcome.
